What WESLR Is

A governed, continuity-first intelligence system for domains that need memory, evidence, and approval.

WESLR Is

  • A governed intelligence system
  • Continuity-first architecture
  • Evidence and provenance aware
  • Operator authority based
  • Designed for domain continuity and traceable work
  • Models are optional capability providers — WESLR is the governed system

WESLR Is Not

  • A chatbot wrapper
  • Certified intelligence
  • Official Turing-test ready
  • Benchmark-proven
  • Production-ready autonomous runtime
  • Public pilot (yet)
