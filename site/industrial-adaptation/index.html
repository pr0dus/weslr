Industrial Adaptation

WESLR is designed to adapt to specific industrial domains — a future direction, not currently available.

Direction: Domain-Specific Systems

WESLR's architecture is designed to support domain-specific adapters. These adapters can provide specialized context, evidence sources, and governance rules for specific industries.

Status: Domain adaptation is a planned direction. No production domain adapters are currently available.
