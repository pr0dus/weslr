Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 May 2026

Summary

WESLR is a governed, continuity-first intelligence system. This Privacy Policy explains how personal data may be processed when people visit the WESLR website, contact WESLR, apply for pilot access, use invited tester accounts, use future workspace/chat functions, upload files, create artifacts, use future payment/subscription functions, or participate in a future marketplace.

As of this revision, weslr.de is publicly reachable as a static WESLR website. staging.weslr.de and chat.weslr.de are password protected. Public self-registration is not open, the public pilot is not live, no public paid product is live, file uploads are not enabled for public users, and no marketplace is live.

4. Website Delivery, Hosting, Security, and Logs

When a person visits WESLR websites, technical data may be processed to deliver the website, maintain security, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot errors. This may include:

  • IP address
  • Date and time of access
  • Requested URL/path
  • Referrer URL where available
  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system/device type
  • Language settings
  • Request headers and technical metadata
  • Security events and access logs
  • Error logs and performance metadata

Hosting/server provider: Owner-operated server infrastructure currently used for the WESLR website and protected routes.

Purpose: Website delivery, secure operation, fraud/abuse prevention, debugging, staging protection, and infrastructure reliability.

Legal basis: Article 6(1)(f) GDPR — legitimate interests in providing a secure, functional website and protecting WESLR systems.

Retention: Default operational retention target is up to 6 months unless shorter deletion is requested/required or longer retention is required for legal obligations, security, abuse prevention, accounting, disputes, backups, or legal claims.

5. Cloudflare

WESLR currently uses Cloudflare for DNS, domain routing, Cloudflare Tunnel / reverse-proxy routing, HTTPS/TLS-related delivery support, security/abuse protection where enabled, and Cloudflare Email Routing. Cloudflare Analytics is expected to be added for website traffic observation.

Cloudflare processing may include:

  • DNS and routing metadata
  • Visitor IP addresses and request metadata
  • Security logs and abuse-prevention signals
  • Caching metadata if caching is enabled
  • Cloudflare Tunnel or access-control metadata if used
  • Email Routing metadata for emails sent to WESLR addresses
  • Technical logs needed to operate Cloudflare services
  • Cloudflare Analytics or traffic measurement data if enabled

International transfers: Cloudflare may process metadata outside the EU/EEA depending on service configuration. WESLR must confirm applicable Cloudflare Data Processing Addendum, Standard Contractual Clauses, EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework status if relied upon, and any supplementary measures before public launch.

6. Email Contact

People may contact WESLR at:

contact@weslr.de

This address is active via Cloudflare Email Routing and forwards to the owner's private mailbox. The private destination mailbox is not published.

Purposes: Responding to inquiries, pilot interest and invitation discussions, partnership requests, support requests, legal requests, security or abuse reports, business administration.

Legal bases: Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interests), Article 6(1)(b) GDPR (pre-contractual steps or contract performance), Article 6(1)(c) GDPR (legal obligation where applicable).

Retention: Contact emails are generally retained for up to 6 months after the last interaction, unless a business/legal relationship, legal obligation, dispute, security issue, accounting/tax requirement, or legal claim requires longer retention.

7. Pilot Interest and invitation-based access

At this time, no public WESLR pilot is live unless explicitly launched later. Pilot access is expected to be invitation-based. Public self-registration will be blocked initially. The first 50 tester accounts may be created manually by WESLR/operator/admin.

There is no automatic right to access WESLR. Submitting a request or showing interest does not guarantee invitation, account creation, pilot participation, commercial access, or continued access.

Legal bases: Article 6(1)(b) GDPR (pre-contractual steps at the request of the person), Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interests in selecting suitable testers, managing limited pilot capacity, protecting systems, and planning WESLR development).

8. User Accounts

When user accounts are enabled, WESLR may process account data such as: name and/or display name, email address, password hash or authentication credentials, account status, role and permissions, organization or workspace affiliation, invitation status, login metadata, security logs, preferences and settings, billing status if payment features are enabled, account lifecycle events.

Initially, public self-registration should be blocked. The first tester accounts may be created manually by WESLR/operator/admin, for example for the first 50 invited testers.

Inactive accounts: Intended to be scheduled for deletion after 6 months of inactivity. WESLR should send an automatic email warning before inactivity deletion where reasonably possible, with the exact warning period to be implemented before account launch. Deletion remains subject to exceptions for legal obligations, tax/accounting records, marketplace transaction records, security logs, abuse prevention, disputes, backups, and legal claims.

9. Chat and Workspace Functionality

When chat and workspace functionality is enabled, WESLR may process data needed to provide WESLR functionality, including: user prompts/messages, conversation history, workspace/project names, user instructions/preferences, selected context, system outputs, generated drafts, operator decisions or approvals, timestamps, audit logs, feedback, moderation/safety/security flags, technical metadata.

AI/model providers: WESLR is intended to provide configuration possibilities for user-configurable or workspace-configurable AI/model providers through help/wiki or similar documentation. WESLR does not provide one fixed default AI/model provider by default and does not rely on one model provider as its identity. If WESLR later offers any default provider, that provider must be listed and legally reviewed before activation.

Retention: Chat/workspace data is generally retained while the account/workspace is active and is intended to be deleted after account deletion or 6 months of inactivity, subject to user controls, backups, legal obligations, security, marketplace/payment records, disputes, or legal claims.

10. Memory, Continuity, Context, Evidence, and Provenance

WESLR may include memory, continuity, context, evidence, provenance, and approval-record features. These are expected to support continuity over time, maintain context where appropriate, preserve source/provenance relationships, and record operator or user decisions.

WESLR should not remember everything by default. Memory/context retention should be bounded, purposeful, transparent, and controllable.

11. File Uploads

File upload functionality is planned/future unless explicitly enabled. Files should not be uploaded unless the feature is active and users have accepted applicable Terms of Use and this Privacy Policy.

Storage provider: Owner-operated server infrastructure unless a future storage provider is added after review.

12. Artifact Creation

Artifact creation is planned/future unless explicitly enabled. WESLR may support creation, editing, storage, export, download, sharing, or publication of artifacts such as: generated documents, reports, code snippets, images or diagrams if supported, spreadsheets, presentations, project outputs, templates, marketplace-ready items if supported.

Artifacts created by users belong to the user or other applicable rights-holder, subject to WESLR Terms of Use, Marketplace Terms, license choices, third-party rights, and applicable law.

13. Marketplace

Marketplace functionality is planned/future unless explicitly launched. The intended marketplace direction is a place where users can create their own WESLR artifacts/work and offer them for sale or licensing, subject to marketplace rules, artifact rules, license terms, moderation, payment/payout requirements, and applicable law.

14. Payments and Billing

Payment and subscription functionality is planned/future unless explicitly enabled. WESLR should not store full payment card numbers unless explicitly confirmed and legally/security reviewed. Payment method details should normally be handled by a payment provider.

Payment provider: Subject to change / to be confirmed before payment activation.

15. AI/Model Provider Processing

WESLR is not an LLM and not a chatbot wrapper. WESLR is a governed, continuity-first intelligence system. Models/LLMs may be optional capability providers, not WESLR's identity or authority.

AI/model providers: User-configurable or workspace-configurable AI/model providers where supported. WESLR does not provide fixed default providers by default. Any future WESLR-provided defaults must be listed before activation.

16. AI Act / AI Transparency Notice

WESLR is under development/evaluation. Where WESLR enables AI-assisted or AI-enabled features, users should be informed when they are interacting with AI-enabled functionality or receiving AI-assisted outputs where required by applicable law.

WESLR does not claim: official AI certification, official benchmark certification, official Turing test certification, certified intelligence status, production readiness, public pilot availability unless explicitly launched, autonomous runtime authority, or superiority over ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or other systems.

The EU AI Act generally applies from 2 August 2026, with some provisions applying earlier or later.

17. Cookies, Local Storage, and Analytics

The WESLR website may use technical cookies, local storage, session storage, or similar technologies because visitors access the site and because security, routing, authentication, preferences, protected areas, and future account/workspace functions may require local or server-side state.

Current analytics: Cloudflare Analytics is planned/expected for WESLR website traffic observation. No non-Cloudflare analytics or marketing trackers are intended unless separately approved.

20. International Transfers

Some providers may process personal data outside the EU/EEA, depending on final provider selection and configuration. This may include Cloudflare, Google/Gmail, payment providers, storage providers, AI/model providers, analytics providers, and security/support providers.

Where personal data is transferred outside the EU/EEA, WESLR should rely on appropriate safeguards where required, such as: EU adequacy decisions, Standard Contractual Clauses, Data Processing Agreements, EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework participation where applicable, supplementary technical and organizational measures, data localization settings where available and appropriate.

22. User Controls

When account/workspace features are enabled, WESLR should provide appropriate user controls, which may include: account settings and profile editing, password/authentication management, export requests, deletion requests, workspace and file deletion, artifact deletion, memory/context viewing, editing, deletion, or disabling where supported, marketplace listing removal, sharing/publication controls, email unsubscribe or withdrawal where optional communications exist, support contact via contact@weslr.de.

Implementation status: User controls to be confirmed before account/workspace launch. Until self-service controls are implemented, requests may be submitted to contact@weslr.de.

24. Security

WESLR uses or plans to use technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal data. These may include: HTTPS/TLS, protected staging access, access controls, role/permission controls for accounts and workspaces, restricted admin access, logging and auditing, security monitoring, malware/security scanning for uploads where enabled, backups and recovery practices, provider security measures, least-privilege operational practices where implemented.

No online service can guarantee absolute security. Users should use strong passwords, protect account credentials, avoid uploading unnecessary sensitive data, and report suspected security issues to contact@weslr.de.

25. Children

WESLR is not directed to children. WESLR should not intentionally create accounts for children or process children's data unless a specific lawful basis, age threshold, parental/guardian process, and product safeguards are confirmed.

Minimum age: 16+

26. Sensitive Data

Users should not upload or provide special-category personal data unless a specific feature or use case permits it, a lawful basis exists, and additional safeguards are in place.

Special-category data may include personal data revealing or concerning: race or ethnic origin, political opinions, religious or philosophical beliefs, trade union membership, genetic data, biometric data used to uniquely identify a person, health data, sex life or sexual orientation.

Users should avoid including unnecessary sensitive data in prompts, emails, uploads, files, artifacts, marketplace listings, or support messages.

27. User Responsibility

Users are responsible for ensuring that they have the necessary rights and lawful basis to upload, submit, generate, share, publish, sell, or otherwise process content through WESLR.

Users must not:

  • Upload personal data of others without authority or lawful basis
  • Upload confidential, sensitive, trade secret, regulated, or unlawful data without authority
  • Upload malware or harmful code
  • Use WESLR to violate rights of others
  • Publish marketplace listings, artifacts, or content that are unlawful, misleading, infringing, or unauthorized
  • Misrepresent WESLR outputs as official certification, legal advice, medical advice, financial advice, government approval, official benchmark result, or verified intelligence/Turing certification unless specifically and lawfully confirmed
