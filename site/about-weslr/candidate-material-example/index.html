Example: Evaluating a Candidate Material Idea

Public-safe material research workflow — candidate idea, hypothesis, validation.

This is a public-safe example of a material research workflow. It shows how WESLR can help explore candidate material ideas — not a production feature or confirmed discovery.

This example uses domain-specific adapters that may be available in future releases. It demonstrates the conceptual workflow, not guaranteed functionality.

Status: Domain-specific adapters are a planned direction. No production adapters are currently available. This example is for illustration only.

Candidate Material Workflow

1. Rough idea → Testable hypothesis

Start with a candidate material idea and formulate a testable hypothesis about its properties or behavior.

2. Prior-art / novelty check

Review existing literature and prior art to establish novelty and identify gaps in current knowledge.

3. Missing evidence map

Identify what evidence is needed to support or refute the hypothesis — gaps in data or understanding.

4. Plausible / uncertain / unsupported separation

Distinguish between supported claims, uncertain assumptions, and unsupported speculation.

5. Next validation step

Define the next experiment, simulation, or analysis that would validate or invalidate the hypothesis.

6. Reasoning trail preserved

Document the entire reasoning chain — assumptions, evidence, gaps, and next steps — for review and continuation.

This example illustrates a conceptual workflow — not a confirmed feature. The material research domain adapter is planned but not currently available.

Important: This is a candidate example, not a production-ready workflow. No material performance is confirmed or guaranteed.
