Example Scenarios

These are designed use cases — not claims of live deployment.

Engineering Project Continuity

A team works on a complex engineering project. Over months, team members change. WESLR maintains context about decisions, constraints, and rationale.

Status: Designed, not deployed.

Research Knowledge Base

A research group accumulates knowledge across experiments. WESLR tracks evidence, hypotheses, and experimental results with provenance.

Status: Designed, not deployed.

Governed Team Memory

A team needs collective memory for decisions, policies, and context. What WESLR remembers is approved by operators. Audit trails are preserved.

Status: Designed, not deployed.

Not a Universal Solution

WESLR is not designed for every use case. It is designed for domains where governance, continuity, and evidence matter.

Important: WESLR is in internal evaluation. No official test results claimed. No production guarantee. Pilot access by invitation.
