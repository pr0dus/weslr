Review/report only. Bounded exploratory posture. No implementation, no schema creation, no solver patching, no runtime wiring, no provider/model calls, no public ARC/Kaggle ingestion, no benchmark/public claims, no learned scorer implementation, no final-answer generation, no `.weslr` writes, and no push.

## 1. Executive verdict
`valid_bounded_exploratory_design_direction`

The Micro-Theory Solver direction is a valid WESLR discovery path precisely because it does **not** reduce unknown-level interpretation to ordinary primitive search. It stays within hard project boundaries while opening a stranger internal reasoning lane. The correct posture is not “safe-only.” The correct posture is **bounded_exploratory_normal**: externally blocked, internally ambitious.

## 3. What is strong

### why this is more unique than primitive/program search
It changes the unit of reasoning. Instead of asking which program maps cells to cells, it asks which temporary explanatory language makes the task lawful. That is materially different from adding more symbolic programs to an existing search loop.

### why temporary-language invention matters
The current UNKNOWN ENGINE direction is explicitly designed to generate strangeness pressure. A solver that cannot invent intermediate representational vocabulary will tend to either overfit familiar cues or fail by saying the task is unsupported. Temporary-language invention gives the system a way to reduce novelty without pretending the correct representation was known in advance.

### why “any bounded form that does better” is valuable
This is one of the strongest parts of the design. It preserves originality. It prevents premature collapse into “symbolic rules only.” It also keeps open hybrid representational forms that may be more natural for absence, contradiction, role migration, or causal pressure than plain textual symbols.

### why role/absence/exception reasoning matters
These are exactly the areas where fixed primitive search tends to become brittle. Unknown levels often hinge not on visible features alone, but on relational function, missing structure, one-object exception behavior, or negative evidence.

### why safe failure matters
Safe failure is a strength, not a retreat. It allows the solver to distinguish representation failure from search-depth failure and avoids false confidence when multiple grammars remain live.

### why UNKNOWN ENGINE levels are the right pressure source
UNKNOWN ENGINE is already structured as a bounded synthetic novelty ladder. That makes it the correct internal gym for a solver whose goal is to become better at inventing temporary languages under controlled strangeness pressure.

## 4. What should become stranger
This design should not only be protected from risk. It should also be pushed toward more ambitious internal structure.

Recommended areas for more strangeness:
- richer private representations beyond symbolic role labels
- non-human-readable but inspectable forms
- explicit pressure fields over scenes and relations
- contradiction maps that track incompatible explanations
- role migration structures across train pairs
- stronger causal/incidental separation machinery
- absence as first-class explanatory structure, not metadata garnish
- rival grammar ecosystems rather than a single candidate stack
- bounded representation mutation between grammar candidates
- micro-theory compression pressure as a first-class ranking signal

The review should explicitly protect the right to explore weird internal forms. Strange is acceptable. Unbounded and uninspectable is not.

## 5. What is vague
The current design is strong directionally, but several things remain operationally vague:

- what “language” means in a recordable operational sense
- how concept invention is bounded without killing creativity
- how strangeness reduction is measured without turning too quickly into a learned scorer
- how inevitability is compared across rivals while staying conceptual-only
- how private grammars remain inspectable if they are not human-readable
- how competing grammars are compared without overfitting to narrative quality
- how representation mutation is allowed without concept explosion
- how to distinguish genuine compression from elegant-sounding description

These are not reasons to stop. They are reasons to define the next bounded artifact carefully.

## 7. Overclaim risk review
Potential wording that could be misread:
- “invents the smallest language” could be misread as generalized intelligence rather than bounded task representation
- “makes the unknown case non-strange” could be misread as broad competence rather than a local representational objective
- “understanding begins” could be read too grandly if surfaced outside internal design context
- inevitability language could be misread as correctness guarantee rather than an internal ranking concept

Safer internal wording when needed:
- “bounded temporary task representation” instead of “understanding engine”
- “internal explanatory compression” instead of “intelligence gain”
- “synthetic-level interpretation hypothesis” instead of “real reasoning breakthrough”
- “conceptual inevitability ranking” instead of “truth score” or “general understanding score”

The current design is not unsafe, but future artifacts should keep this wording discipline explicit.

## 11. Temporary-language flexibility review
The statement
**“The temporary language can take any form as long as it does a better job.”**
is valid as a WESLR discovery direction.

Clarification:
- this is allowed as internal research direction
- it must become bounded, inspectable, serializable, and testable before implementation
- it should not be forced into only human-readable symbolic rules
- strange internal forms should not be rejected merely because they are unfamiliar
- forms should be rejected only when they cannot be inspected, bounded, compared, or tested

This is the key balance: freedom of internal form, discipline of reviewability.
