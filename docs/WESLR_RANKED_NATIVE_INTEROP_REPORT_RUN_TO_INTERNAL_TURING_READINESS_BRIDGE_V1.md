This document defines a design-only bridge from the completed ranked/native interop shadow artifact report-run result toward a future governed internal Turing-style readiness path.

It must not claim:

- official benchmark status;
- IQ or certification status;
- product readiness;
- consciousness or sentience;
- universal human equivalence;
- production deployment readiness.

It is separate from external or official evaluation.
