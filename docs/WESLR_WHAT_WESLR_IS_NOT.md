# WESLR: What WESLR is not

**Date:** 2026-06-07  
**Purpose:** Clear boundaries for claims, capabilities, and expectations

---

## Executive Summary

WESLR is a governed intelligence system with strict claim boundaries. This wiki page explains what WESLR is explicitly **not** — to prevent overclaims and set honest expectations.

These boundaries reflect the operator discussion about cognition, benchmark participation, symbolic emergence, and the line between measurable AI capability and pseudoscience.

---

## WESLR is not

### AGI or consciousness
WESLR is not artificial general intelligence. WESLR is not conscious. WESLR is not beyond science or undiscovered intelligence.

### Certified or benchmark-proven
WESLR does not claim certified intelligence. WESLR does not claim benchmark proof or public launch readiness. WESLR is in internal evaluation.

### Production-ready or open-public
WESLR is not production-ready. WESLR is not open-public. No public pilot is live. Pilot access is by invitation only.

### An open unauthenticated chat
WESLR is not an open public chat. WESLR does not support unauthenticated access. Protected staging and chat routes remain password protected.

### A guaranteed-answer machine
WESLR does not guarantee answers. WESLR does not claim live retrieval or provider execution. Responses are bounded by evidence and operator approval.

### A system that exposes secrets
WESLR does not expose hidden prompts. WESLR does not expose `.weslr` state. WESLR does not expose secrets, API keys, or raw private data.

---

## What WESLR IS (for context)

WESLR **is**:
- A governed intelligence system
- Continuity-first architecture
- Evidence and provenance aware
- Operator authority based
- Designed for domain continuity and traceable work
- Models are optional capability providers — WESLR is the governed system
