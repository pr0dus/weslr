# POST_STEP_SUGGESTION_STANDARD_v1

## Purpose
Every meaningful implementation or activation step must end with a structured suggestion review to (a) surface follow-ups for existing layers and (b) identify gaps or new ideas beyond current layers. This keeps progress continuous without silent redesigns, maintains doctrine-first alignment, and ensures governance-aware activation planning.

## Required Post-Step Review
Cover both categories:
- **A) Existing Layers / Dormant Activation Opportunities:** Improvements, hardening, or activation of already-defined layers. No redesign; align with existing doctrine and boundaries.
- **B) Missing Gaps / New Ideas Beyond Current Layers:** Net-new concepts or capabilities not yet covered. Must remain proposals only, doctrine-first if pursued.

## Suggestion Format (per suggestion)
- **Title** — concise name.
- **Why it matters** — operator-facing value/risk reduction.
- **Existing vs New** — flag as existing-layer follow-up or net-new gap.
- **Risk level** — low/medium/high.
- **Doctrine-first needed** — yes/no (for new work, defaults to yes).
- **Activation vs Net-New** — activation/hardening of existing vs new capability.
- **Approval needed** — yes/no (per governance/role scope).

## Governance Rule
Suggestions are advisory only. They do **not** authorize redesign or activation. Any activation or new work must pass doctrine-first, role/permission checks, and approval per the Governed Activation & Permission Framework.

## Next Step
Apply this standard after each activation/implementation step to capture follow-ups and gaps with governance context.
