# Einkommensteuer (Income Tax) - Germany

**Base Law:** Einkommensteuergesetz (EStG)  
**Latest Amendment:** 2025  
**Application:** Personal income tax for natural persons

## Overview

Einkommensteuer (Income Tax) is levied on the income of natural persons in Germany. It is a personal, progressive tax that applies to all types of income.

### Key Concepts

- **Progressive Taxation:** Tax rates increase with income level
- **Personal Allowances:** Tax-free basic allowance (Grundfreibetrag)
- **Deductions:** Business expenses, special expenses, extraordinary burdens
- **Filing Requirement:** Annual tax return (Steuererklärung)

## Taxpayers

- **Natural persons** residing in Germany
- **Non-residents** with German-source income

## Income Categories (Einkommensarten)

According to §2 EStG, income is categorized into:

1. **Einkünfte aus nichtselbständiger Arbeit** (Wages and salaries)
2. **Einkünfte aus selbständiger Arbeit** (Freelance/Professional income)
3. **Einkünfte aus Gewerbebetrieb** (Business income)
4. **Einkünfte aus Land- und Forstwirtschaft** (Agricultural and forestry)
5. **Einkünfte aus Gewerbebetrieb** (Commercial business)
6. **Einkünfte aus Kapitalvermögen** (Capital income - interest, dividends)
7. **Einkünfte aus Vermietung und Verpachtung** (Rental income)
8. **Sonstige Einkünfte** (Other income)

## Tax Rates

### Income Tax Brackets (2025)

| Tax Rate | Tax Free Threshold | Taxable Range |
|----------|-------------------|---------------|
| 0% | €0 | €0 - €11,604 (basic allowance) |
| 14% - 42% | €11,604 | €11,605 - €62,811 (progressive) |
| 42% | €62,811 | €62,812 - €277,825 |
| 45% | €277,825 | €277,826+ (top rate) |

### Additional Rates

- **Solidarity Surcharge (Solidaritätszuschlag):** 5.5% on income tax (phased out for most taxpayers)
- **Church Tax (Kirchensteuer):** 8-9% on income tax (depending on state)

## Tax-Free Allowances

### Basic Allowance (Grundfreibetrag) 2025

- **Individual:** €11,604
- **Married (joint filing):** €23,208

### Other Allowances

- **Child Allowance (Kindergeld):** €250-€300 per child/month
- **Special Expenses (Sonderausgaben):** €1,000 per year (flat rate)
- **Extraordinary Burdens (außergewöhnliche Belastungen):** Case-by-case

## Deductions

### Werbungskosten (Business Expenses)

- **General Rule:** 3% of income up to €1,500 (flat rate) or actual expenses
- **Professional Expenses:** Up to €1,230 flat rate (2025)
- **Home Office:** €3/day (max €660/year) or actual costs

### Sonderausgaben (Special Expenses)

- **Religious Contributions:** Fully deductible
- **Charitable Donations:** Up to 20% of total income
- **Insurance Premiums:** Health, pension, accident insurance

### Außergewöhnliche Belastungen (Extraordinary Burdens)

- **Medical Expenses:** Threshold: 5-10% of income
- **Alimony:** Fully deductible with court order
- **Disability Costs:** Full deduction with proof

## Tax Return (Steuererklärung)

### Filing Requirements

- **Mandatory:** If income exceeds €11,604 (single) or €23,208 (married)
- **Voluntary:** Even if below threshold (for deduction claims)

### Deadlines

- **Standard:** Next year by May 31
- **With Tax Advisor:** Next year by December 31
- **Late Filing:** Penalties apply

## Tax Classes (Steuerklassen)

| Class | Description |适用人群 |
|-------|-------------|---------|
| I | Single, divorced, widowed | Individuals |
| II | Single with child | Single parents |
| III | Married, high earner | Spouse with lower/no income |
| IV | Married, similar income | Both spouses work |
| V | Married, low earner | Spouse with high income |
| VI | Multiple jobs | Additional jobs |

## Tax Assessment

### Process

1. **Tax Return Submission** (Steuererklärung)
2. **Tax Office Review** (Steuerprüfung)
3. **Tax Assessment Notice** (Steuerfestsetzung)
4. **Tax Payment** (Steuerzahlung)
5. **Refund or Additional Payment**

### Timeline

- **Review Period:** 3-6 months
- **Refund Processing:** 4-8 weeks after assessment

## International Considerations

### Non-Residents

- **Limited Tax Liability:** Only German-source income
- **Tax Treaties:** Germany has 90+ tax treaties

### Expats

- **Impatriate Regime:** 50% tax exemption for 5 years (certain cases)
- **Tax Residency:** 183+ days in Germany = resident
