# Erbschaftsteuer (Inheritance Tax) - Germany

**Base Law:** Erbschaftsteuergesetz (ErbStG)  
**Latest Amendment:** 2025  
**Application:** Inheritance and gift tax

## Overview

Erbschaftsteuer (Inheritance Tax) and Schenkungsteuer (Gift Tax) are levied on the transfer of wealth in Germany. The tax applies to both inheritances and lifetime gifts.

### Key Concepts

- **Progressive Rates:** 7%-50% based on value and relationship
- **Family Exemptions:** Higher tax-free allowances for close relatives
- **Business Exemptions:** Special relief for business assets
- **Spousal Exemption:** Unlimited exemption for spouses

## Taxpayers

### Erbberechtigte (Heirs)

- **Statutory heirs** (gesetzliche Erben)
- **Testamentary heirs** (testamentarische Erben)
- **Beneficiaries** (Vermächtnisempfänger)

### Schenkungsempfänger (Donation Recipients)

- **Private gifts** (privat)
- **Business gifts** (gewerbsmäßig)

## Tax Categories (Steuerklassen)

### Class I - Closest Relatives

| Relationship | Tax-Free Allowance | Rate Range |
|-------------|-------------------|-----------|
| **Spouse/Partner** (Ehegatte/Lebenspartner) | €500,000 | 7%-30% |
| **Children** (Kinder) | €400,000 | 7%-30% |
| **Grandchildren** (Enkelkinder) | €200,000 | 7%-30% |
| **Parents** (Eltern) | €100,000 | 7%-30% |

### Class II - Siblings and Grandparents

| Relationship | Tax-Free Allowance | Rate Range |
|-------------|-------------------|-----------|
| **Siblings** (Geschwister) | €20,000 | 15%-43% |
| **Grandparents** (Großeltern) | €20,000 | 15%-43% |
| **Nieces/Nephews** (Nichten/Neffen) | €20,000 | 15%-43% |

### Class III - All Others

| Relationship | Tax-Free Allowance | Rate Range |
|-------------|-------------------|-----------|
| **Friends, unrelated** (Freunde, Unverwandte) | €20,000 | 30%-50% |
| **Entities** (Körperschaften) | €20,000 | 30%-50% |

## Tax-Free Allowances (Freibeträge)

### Basic Allowances

| Recipient | Allowance | Notes |
|-----------|-----------|-------|
| **Spouse/Partner** | €500,000 | Must be German resident |
| **Children** | €400,000 | Including adopted children |
| **Grandchildren** | €200,000 | Per grandchild |
| **Parents/Grandparents** | €100,000 | Per parent/grandparent |
| **Siblings** | €20,000 | Per sibling |
| **Others** | €20,000 | Per recipient |

### Additional Allowances

| Allowance | Amount | Conditions |
|-----------|--------|-----------|
| **Child allowance** | €7,000 | Per child under 27 (education) |
| **Disability allowance** | €60,000 | Severely disabled recipients |
| **Housing allowance** | €200,000 | Primary residence (certain conditions) |

### Business Exemptions (GewerbeFreistellung)

| Asset Type | Exemption | Conditions |
|------------|-----------|-----------|
| **Business assets** | 100% | Continued operation for 7 years |
| **Agricultural land** | 100% | Continued agricultural use for 7 years |
| **Forest land** | 100% | Continued forest use for 7 years |
| **Shares in companies** | 100% | Company size and activity requirements |

## Tax Rates (Progressive)

### Class I Rates (2025)

| Tax-Free Base | Rate | Example |
|---------------|------|---------|
| Up to €75,000 | 7% | Small inheritance |
| €75,001 - €300,000 | 11% | Medium inheritance |
| €300,001 - €600,000 | 15% | Large inheritance |
| €600,001 - €2,600,000 | 19% | Very large |
| €2,600,001 - €5,200,000 | 23% | Extra large |
| €5,200,001 - €26,000,000 | 27% | Ultra large |
| Over €26,000,000 | 30% | Maximum rate |

### Class II Rates

| Tax-Free Base | Rate |
|---------------|------|
| Up to €75,000 | 15% |
| Over €75,000 | 19%-43% |

### Class III Rates

| Tax-Free Base | Rate |
|---------------|------|
| Up to €75,000 | 30% |
| Over €75,000 | 30%-50% |

## Tax Base (Bemessungsgrundlage)

### Taxable Estate (Steuerliches Vermögen)

**Includes:**
- Real estate (immobilien)
- Financial assets (Geldvermögen)
- Business assets (Gewerbevermögen)
- Personal property (Mobiliar)
- Life insurance proceeds
- Trust assets

**Excludes:**
- Debts and liabilities
- funeral expenses
- Estate administration costs

### Valuation

| Asset Type | Valuation Method |
|------------|-----------------|
| **Real estate** | Market value (Verkehrswert) |
| **Shares** | Market value or book value |
| **Business assets** | Enterprise value |
| **Financial assets** | Market value at death date |
| **Personal property** | Fair market value |

## Gift Tax (Schenkungsteuer)

### Same Rates as Inheritance Tax

- **Class I, II, III** rates apply identically
- **Same allowances** as inheritance tax
- **Same exemptions** for business assets

### Lifetime Gifts

- **Total gifts within 10 years** aggregated
- **10-year rule:** Gifts within 10 years of death included

### Reporting

- **Deadline:** 1 month from gift date
- **Form:** Schenkungserklärung
- **Tax office:** Local Finanzamt

## Filing Requirements

### Inheritance Tax Return (Erbschaftsteuererklärung)

**Who Must File:**
- All heirs (Erben)
- Beneficiaries (Vermächtnisempfänger)
- Estate administrators (Testamentsvollstrecker)

**Deadline:**
- **6 months** from knowledge of inheritance
- **Extension:** Upon request (typically 3 months)

**Required Documents:**
- Death certificate ( Sterbeurkunde)
- Will (Testament) or intestacy documents
- Asset list and valuations
- Debts and liabilities

## Tax Payment

### Payment Terms

- **Deadline:** Within 6 months of tax assessment
- **Installments:** Available for large estates
- **Interest:** 5% per annum on late payments

### Payment Options

- **Lump sum:** Full payment
- **Installments:** Up to 10 years (with interest)
- **Asset transfer:** In-kind payment (real estate, shares)

## Enforcement

### Audits (Erbschaftsteuer-Prüfungen)

- **Scope:** 10 years retroactive
- **Focus:** Undisclosed assets, valuation disputes
- **Penalties:** Up to 25% of tax due

### Penalty Rates

| Violation | Penalty |
|-----------|---------|
| **Late filing** | Up to 10% of tax due |
| **Underdeclaration** | 10%-25% of underpaid tax |
| **Intentional evasion** | Criminal prosecution |

## International Considerations

### Foreign Assets

- **Taxable in Germany** if German resident
- **Foreign tax credit:** Available for foreign inheritance tax
- **Tax treaties:** Germany has 30+ treaties

### Non-Residents

- **Limited tax liability:** German-situated assets only
- **Real estate:** Always taxable
- **Bank accounts:** Taxable if held by German residents

## Special Exemptions

### Primary Residence (Wohnung)

- **Exemption:** Up to €200,000 (value)
- **Conditions:** Heir must live in property for 10 years
- **Relocation:** Allowed for reasonable distance

### Agricultural and Forest Land

- **100% exemption** if continued use
- **7-year continuity requirement**
- **Size requirements:** Minimum 5 hectares

### Charitable Bequests

- **100% exemption** for qualified charities
- **Requirements:** Registered non-profit status
- **Documentation:** Proof of charitable purpose

## Planning Strategies

### Lifetime Gifts

- **Annual gifts:** Use tax-free allowance (€20,000)
- **Spousal gifts:** €500,000 tax-free
- **Grandchildren gifts:** €200,000 tax-free

### Trusts and Foundations

- **Stiftung (foundation):** Long-term estate planning
- **Vertrauen (trust):** Asset protection
- **GmbH (limited):** Business succession

### Business Succession

- **GewerbeFreistellung:** 100% exemption for business assets
- **7-year retention requirement**
- **Management continuity**
