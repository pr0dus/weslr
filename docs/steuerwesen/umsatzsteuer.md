# Umsatzsteuer (VAT) - Germany

**Base Law:** Umsatzsteuergesetz (UStG)  
**Latest Amendment:** 2025  
**Application:** Value-added tax on goods and services

## Overview

Umsatzsteuer (VAT) is a consumption tax on goods and services in Germany. It's a multi-stage tax that applies at each point of production and distribution.

### Key Concepts

- **Multi-Stage Tax:** Applied at each transaction in supply chain
- **Input Tax Credit (Vorsteuer):** Businesses can deduct VAT paid on inputs
- **Standard Rate:** 19% (reduced rate: 7%)
- **Reverse Charge:** VAT liability shifted to recipient (certain services)

## Taxpayers

- **Natural persons** and **legal entities** engaged in economic activities
- **Small businesses** (Kleinunternehmer) may be exempt

## Tax Rates

### Standard and Reduced Rates (2025)

| Rate | Description |适用范围 |
|------|-------------|---------|
| 19% | Standard rate | Most goods and services |
| 7% | Reduced rate | Food, books, newspapers, public transport, hospitality |

### Special Rates

- **0%:** International transport, exports to non-EU countries
- **0%:** Intra-EU supplies (under certain conditions)

## Taxable Events

### Umsatzsteuerpflichtiger Umsatz (Taxable Supply)

- **Delivery of goods** (Warenverkäufe)
- **Provision of services** (Dienstleistungen)
- **Leasing and letting** (Vermietung)
- **Import of goods** (Einfuhr)
- **Intra-EU acquisitions** (Innergemeinschaftliche Erwerbe)

### Exemptions (Steuerfreie Umsätze)

- **Medical services** (arts, dentists, physiotherapists)
- **Educational services** (schools, universities)
- **Financial services** (banking, insurance)
- **Postal services** (traditional mail)
- **Cultural services** (museums, theaters - certain conditions)

## Input Tax Deduction (Vorsteuerabzug)

### Requirements

1. **Valid invoice** (Umsatzsteuer-Identifikationsnummer)
2. **Business use** (gewerblicher Nutzung)
3. **Time of deduction** (Steuerzeitpunkt)

### Deduction Timeline

- **When received:** Invoice date or payment date (whichever is earlier)
- **Deadline:** 4 years from end of tax period

### Partial Deduction

- **Mixed use:** Pro-rata deduction based on business vs private use
- **Threshold:** Less than €1,000/year = full deduction allowed

## Invoices (Rechnungen)

### Required Information

1. **Seller's VAT ID** (Umsatzsteuer-Identifikationsnummer)
2. **Buyer's VAT ID** (if applicable)
3. **Invoice number** (sequential)
4. **Date of issue** (Rechnungsdatum)
5. **Date of supply** (Leistungsdatum)
6. **Description of goods/services**
7. **Tax rate and amount** (Nettobetrag + Umsatzsteuer)
8. **Total amount** (Bruttobetrag)

###电子发票 (电子Rechnung)

- **Mandatory since 2024:** For B2G (business-to-government)
- **2027:** Extended to B2B (business-to-business)

## VAT Returns (Umsatzsteuer-Voranmeldung)

### Filing Frequency

| Condition | Filing Frequency |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Annual turnover > €1,000,000 | Quarterly |
| Annual turnover ≤ €1,000,000 | Quarterly (voluntary monthly) |
| New businesses | Monthly until turnover exceeds threshold |
| Small businesses (Kleinunternehmer) | No filing required |

### Deadlines

- **Quarterly:** End of month following quarter
- **Monthly:** 10th day of following month
- **Extension:** Upon request (typically 1 month)

## Small Business Regulation (Kleinunternehmerregelung)

### Conditions

- **Turnover < €22,000** in previous year
- **Projected turnover < €22,000** in current year
- **Opt-out possible** (must file Form 08/1)

### Advantages

- **No VAT collection** (simpler accounting)
- **No VAT returns** (reduced administrative burden)
- **No VAT registration required**

### Disadvantages

- **No input tax deduction** (cannot reclaim VAT on business expenses)
- **No VAT ID** (may limit B2B clients)

## Intra-EU Transactions

### Dispatch and Acquisition

- **Dispatch (Versand):** Move goods from Germany to EU
- **Acquisition (Erwerb):** Move goods from EU to Germany

### Rules

- **Zero-rated** (0% VAT) if VAT ID provided
- **VAT due** in destination country
- ** Intracommunity Supplies Declaration** ( Intracommunity Supplies Declaration)

### Distance Selling

- **Threshold:** €10,000/year to single EU country
- **OSS (One-Stop Shop):** Optional VAT registration in home country

## Reverse Charge (Umkehrung der Steuerschuld)

### Scenarios

- **B2B construction services**
- **B2B precious metals**
- **B2B mobile phones and chips**
- **B2B waste and scrap**

### Process

1. **Supplier** invoices net amount (no VAT)
2. **Customer** self-assesses VAT
3. **Customer** reports VAT in VAT return

## Special Schemes

### Margin Scheme (Differenzbesteuerung)

- **Used for:** Used goods, art, antiques, collectibles
- **Taxed on:** Profit margin, not full value

### Tour Operators Scheme

- **Used for:** Package holidays
- **Taxed on:** Margin (difference between selling price and costs)

## Enforcement

### Audits (Umsatzsteuer-Prüfungen)

- **Scope:** 3-4 years retroactive
- **Focus:** Invoice documentation, VAT identification, deduction claims

### Penalties

- **Late filing:** Up to 10% of tax due
- **Underdeclaration:** Interest + penalty (1-25%)
- **Intentional evasion:** Criminal penalties
