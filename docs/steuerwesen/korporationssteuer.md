# Körperschaftsteuer (Corporate Tax) - Germany

**Base Law:** Körperschaftsteuergesetz (KStG)  
**Latest Amendment:** 2025  
**Application:** Corporate income tax for legal entities

## Overview

Körperschaftsteuer (Corporate Income Tax) is levied on the income of legal entities in Germany. It applies to corporations, partnerships, and other entities with legal personality.

### Key Concepts

- **Flat Rate:** 25% on taxable income
- **Solidarity Surcharge:** 5.5% on KSt (phased out for most)
- **Imputation System:** Dividends taxed at effective rate
- **Group Relief:** Loss offsetting within groups

## Taxpayers

### Fully Taxable (Unbeschränkt Steuerpflichtig)

- **Legal persons** with registered office in Germany
- **Partnership firms** (Gesellschaften mit bürgerlichem Recht)
- **Institutions** (foundations, associations)

### Partially Taxable (Beschränkt Steuerpflichtig)

- **Foreign entities** with German-source income
- **Non-resident companies** with German operations

## Taxable Entities

### Corporations

| Entity Type | Tax Status | Notes |
|------------|------------|-------|
| **GmbH** (Limited) | Fully taxable | Most common structure |
| **AG** (Stock Corp) | Fully taxable | Public companies |
| **UG** (Mini GmbH) | Fully taxable | €1 minimum capital |
| **e.V.** (Non-profit) | Partial exemption | Registered association |
| **OHG** (Partnership) | Pass-through | Income taxed at partner level |

### Pass-Through Entities

- **Gewerbebetrieb** (Business operations)
- **Kapitalgesellschaft** (Capital companies)
- **Personengesellschaft** (Partnerships)

## Tax Base (Bemessungsgrundlage)

### Taxable Income (Körperschaftseinkommen)

1. **Business income** (Gewerbe Einkommen)
2. **Capital income** (Kapitaleinkommen)
3. **Rental income** (Vermietungseinkommen)
4. **Other income** (Sonstiges Einkommen)

### Adjustments

- **Depreciation:** Tax-allowed methods only
- **Loss carryforwards:** Unlimited (100% offset)
- **Loss carrybacks:** 1 year (100% offset)

## Tax Rates

### Standard Rate (2025)

| Tax | Rate | Applied to |
|-----|------|-----------|
| **Körperschaftsteuer** | 25% | Taxable income |
| **Solidarity Surcharge** | 0% | Phased out for most taxpayers |
| **Trade Tax** | 14-17% | Grosse business income |

### Effective Tax Rate

- **Combined rate (KSt + GewSt):** 30-33%
- **Dividend relief:** 5% effective rate on qualifying dividends

## Tax Holidays and Incentives

### Research & Development

- **R&D Tax Credit:** 25% of eligible R&D costs (up to €4M)
- **Innovation Allowance:** 50% immediate depreciation

### Capital Investments

- **Immediate Depreciation:** Up to €800,000 (2025)
- **Simplified Depreciation:** 20% straight-line

### Regional Incentives

- **Eastern Germany:** Additional 5% investment grant
- **Structural Weak Regions:** Extended tax holidays

## Dividends and Profit Distribution

### Dividend Taxation

| Recipient | Tax Rate | Notes |
|-----------|----------|-------|
| **Individuals** | 25% + solidarity | Withholding tax (Quellensteuer) |
| **Corporations** | 0% (participating interest) | Ownership ≥ 10% for 1 year |
| **Partnerships** | Pass-through | Taxed at partner level |

### Tax Credits

- **Imputation credit:** 5% of dividend (effective 20% tax)
- **Foreign-source dividends:** Full exemption (participation exemption)

## Loss Carryforwards

### Rules

- **Carryforward:** Unlimited duration
- **Offset:** 100% of future income
- **Change of ownership:** Limitation (50% rule)

### Special Rules

- **CARES Act losses:** 100% carryforward (2020-2023)
- **R&D losses:** Additional 25% refundable credit

## Group Relief

### Loss Offset

- **Direct subsidiary:** Full offset allowed
- **Sister companies:** Not allowed
- **Indirect subsidiary:** Allowed (through parent)

### Tax Group (Steuerrechtliche Gruppe)

- **Requirements:** 100% ownership, same tax year
- **Benefits:** Loss offset, unified assessment
- **Duration:** Minimum 5 years

## Withholding Tax (Quellensteuer)

### Rates

| Payment Type | Rate | Recipient |
|-------------|------|-----------|
| **Dividends** | 25% | Individuals |
| **Interest** | 25% | Individuals (certain cases) |
| **Royalties** | 25% | Individuals (certain cases) |

### Exemptions

- **EU/EEA residents:** Reduced rates under treaties
- **Corporations:** No withholding (participation exemption)

## Tax Returns

### Filing Requirements

- **Deadline:** May 31 (next year)
- **Extension:** December 31 (with tax advisor)
- **Electronic filing:** Mandatory for corporations

### Required Documents

1. **Tax return form** (KSt 1)
2. **Financial statements** (Bilanz, Gewinn- und Verlustrechnung)
3. **Tax calculation** (Steuerbemessung)
4. **Schedules** (Anlagen A-L)

## Special Schemes

### Sounding Box Regime (Sounding Box Regime)

- **For:** Small and medium-sized enterprises
- **Benefit:** Simplified accounting, reduced compliance burden
- **Turnover limit:** €500,000/year

### Entrepreneurial Succession (Unternehmensnachfolge)

- **Gift tax exemption:** €800,000 (business assets)
- **Inheritance tax exemption:** €800,000 (business assets)
- **Conditions:** 10 years continued operation

## International Taxation

### Cross-Border Transactions

- **PE definition:** Fixed place of business
- **Transfer pricing:** Arm's length principle
- **Withholding taxes:** Reduced by treaties

### CFC Rules (Controlled Foreign Corporation)

- **Applicable if:** German resident controls foreign entity
- **Taxed on:** Passive income (interest, royalties, dividends)

### Hybrid Mismatch Arrangements

- **Goal:** Neutralize tax benefits from timing differences
- **Application:** 2025 onwards (EU ATAD II)

## Enforcement

### Audits (Körperschaftsteuer-Prüfungen)

- **Scope:** 4 years retroactive
- **Focus:** Transfer pricing, loss carryforwards, group relief

### Penalties

- **Late filing:** Up to 10% of tax due
- **Underdeclaration:** Interest + penalty (1-25%)
- **Tax evasion:** Criminal penalties
