# Gewerbesteuer (Business Tax) - Germany

**Base Law:** Gewerbesteuergesetz (GewStG)  
**Latest Amendment:** 2025  
**Application:** Municipal trade tax on business income

## Overview

Gewerbesteuer (Business Tax) is a municipal tax on business income in Germany. It's a key source of revenue for cities and municipalities, and it's integrated with Körperschaftsteuer (corporate tax) and Einkommensteuer (income tax).

### Key Concepts

- **Municipal Tax:** Rates vary by location (Hebesätze)
- **Combined Rate:** Effective rate 14%-17% on business income
- **Loss Carries:** Unlimited carryforwards
- **Integration:** With KSt and ESt via income integration

## Taxpayers

### Gewerbeiberechtigte (Business Taxpayers)

- **Natural persons** engaged in commercial activity
- **Partnerships** (OHG, KG, KGaa)
- **Corporations** (GmbH, AG, UG)
- **Foreign entities** with German business establishment

### Exemptions

- **Freelancers** (Freiberufler) - no Gewerbesteuer
- **Agriculture and forestry** - separate regime
- **Small businesses** below threshold

## Tax Base (Bemessungsgrundlage)

### Taxable Business Income (Gewerbeertrag)

**Calculation:**
```
Gewerbeertrag = Gewinn nach EStG/KStG + Zuschläge - Freibeträge
```

### Adjustments (Zuschläge und Freibeträge)

| Adjustment | Value | Notes |
|-----------|-------|-------|
| **Addback: KSt fraction** | 4% of KSt | Artificial increase |
| **Addback: KSt fraction** | 3.5% of KSt | Alternative calculation |
| **Freibetrag (basic allowance)** | €24,500 | Per business |
| **Freibetrag (new businesses)** | €50,000 | First 3 years |

### Income Integration

**Körperschaftsteuer Income Integration:**
```
Körperschaftsteuer-Einkommen × 3.5% = Gewerbesteuer-Bemessungsgrundlage
```

**Einkommensteuer Income Integration:**
```
Einkommensteuer-Einkommen × 3.5% = Gewerbesteuer-Bemessungsgrundlage
```

## Tax Rates (Hebesätze)

### Municipal Rate Multipliers (2025)

| Location | Hebesatz | Effective Rate |
|----------|----------|---------------|
| **Berlin** | 123% | ~17.2% |
| **Munich** | 173% | ~21.7% |
| **Hamburg** | 129% | ~16.3% |
| **Cologne** | 138% | ~17.6% |
| **Frankfurt** | 140% | ~18.2% |
| **Stuttgart** | 145% | ~18.7% |
| **Düsseldorf** | 130% | ~17.0% |
| **Leipzig** | 135% | ~17.5% |
| **Bonn** | 130% | ~17.0% |
| **Dresden** | 140% | ~18.2% |

### Calculation

**Effective Tax Rate:**
```
Gewerbesteuer = Gewerbeertrag × 3.5% × Hebesatz
```

**Example (Berlin):**
```
€100,000 × 3.5% × 123% = €4,305
```

### Combined Effective Rate (with KSt)

| Location | KSt (25%) | GewSt | Total |
|----------|-----------|-------|-------|
| **Berlin** | €25,000 | €4,305 | €29,305 (29.3%) |
| **Munich** | €25,000 | €6,055 | €31,055 (31.1%) |
| **Low-rate city** | €25,000 | €3,500 | €28,500 (28.5%) |

## Business Types

### Gewerbe (Commercial Business)

- **Definition:** Profit-oriented, ongoing activity
- **Examples:** Retail, manufacturing, services
- **Taxable:** All commercial activities

### Freiberufler (Freelancer)

- **Definition:** Independent professional services
- **Examples:** Doctors, lawyers, architects, consultants
- **Exempt:** No Gewerbesteuer (but subject to ESt/USt)

### Kleingewerbe (Small Business)

- **Definition:** Small-scale commercial activity
- **Threshold:** Below €24,500 profit (Freibetrag applies)
- **Taxable:** On profit above Freibetrag

### Scheduling (Gewerbeanmeldung)

**Required for:**
- All commercial businesses
- Foreign entities with German operations

**Exempt from scheduling:**
- Freelancers (Freiberufler)
- Agricultural/forestry activities
- Certain small businesses (below threshold)

## Loss Carryforwards

### Rules

- **Carryforward:** Unlimited duration
- **Offset:** Against future business income
- **Carryback:** Not allowed (since 2023)

### Special Rules

- **CARES Act losses:** Special treatment for 2020-2023
- **COVID-19 losses:** Extended carryforward (certain cases)

## Tax Returns (Gewerbesteuererklärung)

### Filing Requirements

- **Deadline:** 6 months after fiscal year-end
- **Extension:** Upon request (typically 2 months)
- **Electronic filing:** Mandatory for corporations

### Required Information

1. **Business income** (Gewerbeertrag)
2. **Adjustments** (Zuschläge, Freibeträge)
3. **Tax base** (Bemessungsgrundlage)
4. **Municipal rate** (Hebesatz)
5. **Tax calculation**

## Special Schemes

### Small Business Relief (Kleingewerbe-Freistellung)

**Conditions:**
- Profit below €24,500 (basic Freibetrag)
- No employees or minimal employees
- Non-commercial activity

**Benefits:**
- No Gewerbesteuer
- No Gewerbeanmeldung required

### New Business Relief (Neugründungen)

**Conditions:**
- Business established within last 3 years
- Profit below €50,000 in first years
- New business registration

**Benefits:**
- Higher Freibetrag (€50,000 instead of €24,500)
- Reduced administrative burden

### Loss Carryback (Verlustrechnung)

**Conditions:**
- Significant loss due to COVID-19 (2020-2022)
- Application required
- Limited to specific years

**Benefits:**
- Refund of previous year's tax
- Cash flow improvement

## Enforcement

### Audits (Gewerbesteuer-Prüfungen)

- **Scope:** 4 years retroactive
- **Focus:** Business classification, profit calculation
- **Penalties:** Up to 10% of tax due

### Penalties

| Violation | Penalty |
|-----------|---------|
| **Late filing** | Up to 10% of tax due |
| **Underdeclaration** | 10%-25% of underpaid tax |
| **Business tax evasion** | Criminal prosecution |

## International Considerations

### Foreign Businesses

- **Taxable if** German business establishment
- **Permanent establishment (PE)** definition applies
- **Tax treaties** may reduce liability

### Cross-Border Services

- **Place of供应 (Supply location)** determines taxation
- **Digital services:** User location determines taxation
- **VAT integration:** GewSt with USt coordination
