# Kapitalertragsteuer (Capital Gains Tax) - Germany

**Base Law:** §20-§27 EStG  
**Latest Amendment:** 2025  
**Application:** Tax on capital income and investments

## Overview

Kapitalertragsteuer (Capital Gains Tax) is a flat-rate tax on capital income in Germany. It applies to interest, dividends, and profits from the sale of securities and real estate.

### Key Concepts

- **Flat Rate:** 25% on capital income
- **Solidarity Surcharge:** 5.5% on Kapitalertragsteuer (phased out)
- **Church Tax:** 8-9% on Kapitalertragsteuer
- **Sparer-Pauschbetrag:** Tax-free allowance of €1,000 (single) / €2,000 (married)
- **Withholding Tax (Quellensteuer):** Automatically withheld by banks

## Taxable Events

### Taxable Capital Income (Kapitalerträge)

| Type | Description | Taxation |
|------|-------------|----------|
| **Interest** (Zinsen) | Bank deposits, bonds, loans | 25% + solidarity |
| **Dividends** (Dividenden) | Stock dividends, distributions | 25% + solidarity |
| **Capital Gains** (Kapitalgewinne) | Sale of securities, cryptocurrencies | 25% + solidarity |
| **Fund Income** (Fondserträge) | Investment fund distributions | 25% + solidarity |
| **Rental Income** (Vermietung) | Real estate rental | Progressive tax (not Kapitalertragsteuer) |

### Non-Taxable Capital Income

- **Savings Bonus (Sparprämie):** Up to €200/year (phased out)
- **Government Bonds:** Certain federal bonds exempt
- **Educational Savings:** Specific savings plans exempt

## Tax Rates (2025)

| Tax Component | Rate | Applied to |
|---------------|------|-----------|
| **Kapitalertragsteuer** | 25% | Capital income |
| **Solidarity Surcharge** | 0% | Phased out for most taxpayers |
| **Church Tax** | 8-9% | On Kapitalertragsteuer (depending on state) |

### Effective Rate

- **Standard effective rate:** 25% (without church tax)
- **With church tax:** 27-27.25%

## Tax-Free Allowances

### Sparer-Pauschbetrag (Saver's Basic Allowance)

| Status | Amount | Notes |
|--------|--------|-------|
| **Single** | €1,000/year | Tax-free capital income |
| **Married (joint)** | €2,000/year | Combined allowance |
| **Minors** | €1,000/year | Per child |

### Other Allowances

- **Freibetrag for small savings:** Up to €500 (specific accounts)
- **Pension savings (Riester):** Tax-deferred growth
- **Employee shares:** Tax-free if held 5+ years

## Withholding Tax (Quellensteuer)

### Automatic Withholding

Banks and financial institutions automatically withhold tax on:

- **Interest payments** (Zinsen)
- **Dividend payments** (Dividenden)
- **Fund distributions** (Fondserträge)

### Process

1. **Financial institution** calculates tax due
2. **Withholds tax** at source (25% + solidarity + church tax)
3. **Pays net amount** to investor
4. **Reports to tax office** (automatically)

### Credit for Withheld Tax

- **Tax return:** Can claim credit for withheld tax
- **Overpayment:** Refund if below allowance threshold

## Sale of Securities (Veräußerungsgeschäft)

### Taxable Gain (Veräußerungsertrag)

**Calculation:**
```
Veräußerungsertrag = Veräußerungserlös - Anschaffungskosten - Werbungskosten
```

### Holding Period Rules

| Holding Period | Tax Treatment |
|---------------|---------------|
| **≤ 1 year** | Full gain taxable (25% + solidarity) |
| **> 1 year** | Exempt from Kapitalertragsteuer |
| **> 10 years** | Full exemption (retirement savings) |

### Specific Rules

- **Shares:** Holding period starts at purchase date
- **Funds:** Holding period based on fund shares held
- **Cryptocurrencies:** Treated as private sale (same rules)

## Investment Funds (Investmentfonds)

### Taxation of Fund Distributions

| Distribution Type | Tax Treatment |
|-------------------|---------------|
| **Dividend distributions** | 25% Kapitalertragsteuer |
| **Interest distributions** | 25% Kapitalertragsteuer |
| **Capital gain distributions** | 25% Kapitalertragsteuer |
| **Full distributions** | 25% Kapitalertragsteuer |

### Tax-Optimized Funds

- **ETFs (exchange-traded funds):** Tax-transparent (Germany)
- **UCITS funds:** EU-wide harmonization
- **Special funds:** Specific tax regimes

## Real Estate Capital Gains (Immobilien-Kapitalgewinne)

### General Rule

- **Holding period ≤ 10 years:** Full gain taxable
- **Holding period > 10 years:** Exempt
- **Primary residence:** Special rules (§23 EStG)

### Calculation

```
Steuerpflichtiger Gewinn = Verkaufspreis - Anschaffungskosten -Verbesserungskosten - Werbungskosten
```

### Special Exemptions

- **Primary residence:** Exempt if lived in for 2+ years
- **Inherited property:** Special holding period rules
- **Business assets:** Different rules (Gewerbesteuer may apply)

## Loss Carryforwards

### Rules

- **Offset against capital income** (not against other income)
- **Carryforward:** Unlimited duration
- **Carryback:** Not allowed

### Example

```
Year 1: Capital loss of €5,000
Year 2: Capital gain of €3,000
Result: Loss carryforward of €2,000
```

## Tax Returns (Steuererklärung)

### Reporting Requirements

**Include in tax return:**
- Total capital income
- Interest income (Zinsen)
- Dividend income (Dividenden)
- Capital gains (Kapitalgewinne)
- Fund distributions (Fondserträge)

### Documentation

- **Bank statements** (Zinsbescheinigungen)
- **Broker statements** (Verkaufsbelege)
- **Fund reports** (Ausschüttungsmitteilungen)

## International Considerations

### Foreign Capital Income

- **Taxable in Germany** if resident
- **Foreign tax credit:** Available for foreign withholding tax
- **Tax treaties:** Reduced rates (e.g., US dividends: 15%)

### Non-Residents

- **Limited tax liability:** German-source capital income only
- **Withholding tax:** Applied at source
- **No tax return required** (if only German-source income)
