# WESLR V1 Logo and Icon Concept Brief V1

Date: 2026-05-29
Status: concept / design brief only

## 1. Brand Identity Core

### 1.1 Name Origin

WESLR echoes Romanian/Slavic/Czech forms around **vâslar / veslar / veslař / veslo** — meaning rower, oarsman, or oar.

This gives WESLR a natural symbolic identity:
- It rows through uncertainty
- It keeps direction
- It carries meaning safely
- It is the vessel, not the chaos

### 1.2 Core Metaphor

**WESLR = the rower / vessel / navigator of meaning.**

Not autonomous chaos. Not uncontrolled drift. A governed vessel that navigates uncertainty with direction, evidence, and operator authority.

### 1.3 Brand Positioning

WESLR is:
- Serious but not cold
- Intelligent but not generic AI
- Calm, deliberate, trustworthy
- Governed cognition, not uncontrolled autonomy

WESLR is not:
- A chatbot
- A robot
- A neural network visualization
- Autonomous intelligence
- Generic tech startup

## 2. Logo Concept

### 2.1 Core Idea

A minimal geometric **W** that can be read as:
- Two oars in motion
- A small vessel/hull shape
- Soft waves beneath
- Controlled forward motion

A small **guiding dot/star** above the center of the W represents:
- Operator authority
- Evidence anchor
- Governance
- North-star truth

### 2.2 Logo Meaning

The logo represents:
- **W shape**: The name, but also the vessel
- **Two strokes**: Oars pulling through water
- **Curve at base**: Hull of a boat, vessel shape
- **Guiding dot**: Evidence, authority, direction
- **Overall**: Navigating uncertainty with governance

### 2.3 Tagline Concept

**"WESLR — rowing meaning through uncertainty."**

This captures:
- Movement through uncertain waters
- Direction and purpose
- The vessel metaphor
- Governed navigation

## 4. Visual Variants

### 4.1 Variant A: Minimal W-Oar Mark

**Concept:** Pure geometric W with oar-like strokes.

**Structure:**
- Two angled strokes forming a W
- Strokes widen slightly at top (oar blade shape)
- Narrow at center (oar shaft)
- Widen again at base (oar blade in water)
- Small dot above center

**Visual feel:**
- Clean, precise, deliberate
- Minimal curves
- Strong geometric foundation
- Reads as W immediately

**Use case:**
- Primary logo mark
- App icon
- Large displays

**Monochrome test:**
- Works as solid shape
- Works as outline
- Works at 16×16

---

### 4.2 Variant B: W-Wave Vessel Mark

**Concept:** W shape with subtle vessel and wave suggestion.

**Structure:**
- W strokes curve inward at base (hull shape)
- Bottom edge has subtle wave curve
- Two oar strokes rise from hull
- Guiding dot above
- Entire form suggests vessel floating on water

**Visual feel:**
- Softer, more organic
- Vessel metaphor stronger
- Wave adds movement
- Still reads as W

**Use case:**
- Website header
- Marketing materials
- Where vessel metaphor adds value

**Monochrome test:**
- Hull shape must be clear
- Wave must not disappear
- Oars must remain legible

---

### 4.3 Variant C: W with Guiding Star/Dot

**Concept:** Emphasis on governance dot/star as primary element.

**Structure:**
- Minimal W shape (clean strokes)
- Prominent dot or small star above center
- Dot/star is the focus
- W supports, dot leads

**Visual feel:**
- Authority-forward
- Evidence as north star
- Governance as guiding principle
- W as vessel carrying the star

**Use case:**
- Where governance message is primary
- Enterprise contexts
- Where authority needs emphasis

**Monochrome test:**
- Dot/star must be visible at small sizes
- Must not merge with W strokes
- Must remain distinct

## 5. What to Avoid

### 5.1 Forbidden Visual Elements

| Element | Reason |
|---------|--------|
| Robot heads | Generic AI cliché |
| Neural network brains | Overused, misleading |
| Circuit board patterns | Wrong category (not hardware) |
| Sparkles/glitter | Playful, not serious |
| Chatbot speech bubbles | WESLR is not a chatbot |
| Floating orbs/spheres | Generic tech |
| Gradient meshes | Dated, not timeless |
| Bold neon colors | Playful, not serious |
| 3D effects | Not minimal |
| Shadows/glows | Distracting |

### 5.2 Forbidden Concepts

| Concept | Reason |
|---------|--------|
| Autonomous AI | WESLR is governed, not autonomous |
| Magic intelligence | WESLR is grounded, not magical |
| Speed/motion blur | WESLR is deliberate, not rushed |
| Infinite/loop shapes | WESLR has boundaries |
| Brain imagery | WESLR is not a brain simulation |
| Eye imagery | Surveillance connotation |
| Shield/lock alone | Governance is more than security |

### 5.3 Forbidden Styles

| Style | Reason |
|-------|--------|
| Playful/cartoony | Not serious enough |
| Gradient/iridescent | Dated, not timeless |
| Thin hairline strokes | Fails at small sizes |
| Complex illustrations | Not minimal |
| Photorealistic 3D | Wrong direction |

## 6. Color Direction

### 6.1 Primary Palette

**Base:** Dark navy/charcoal (from UI spec)

| Color | Hex | Usage |
|-------|-----|-------|
| Primary Background | `#0D1117` | Logo on dark background |
| Primary Text | `#F0F6FC` | Logo on light background |
| Accent Blue | `#388BFF` | Primary brand color |

### 6.2 Accent Color Options

**Option 1: Blue Accent** (recommended)
- Primary: `#388BFF` (bright blue)
- Hover: `#58A6FF` (cyan-blue)
- Active: `#1F6FEB` (pressed blue)

**Option 2: Cyan-Blue Accent**
- Primary: `#58A6FF`
- Secondary: `#388BFF`

**Option 3: Teal Accent** (alternative)
- Primary: `#39C5CF`
- Secondary: `#2EA6B0`

### 6.3 Monochrome Usage

- **On dark:** Solid white (`#F0F6FC`)
- **On light:** Solid black (`#0D1117`)
- **On accent:** Solid white

### 6.4 Color Rules

| Context | Color |
|---------|-------|
| Logo on dark | White or accent |
| Logo on light | Dark navy or accent |
| Logo on accent | White |
| Icon small (favicon) | Solid fill, single color |
| Icon medium | Can use accent |
| Icon large | Can use accent with dot highlight |

### 6.5 Color Constraints

- **No gradients** in the logo itself
- **No more than 2 colors** in the logo
- **No glow effects**
- **No transparency** in primary logo
- **Accent is optional** — logo works in monochrome

## 7. Typography Direction

### 7.1 Logo Wordmark

**Font:** Inter (or similar clean sans-serif)

**Weight:**
- Logo wordmark: Semibold (600) or Bold (700)
- Tagline: Regular (400)

**Size relationship:**
- Logo mark: 24–48px depending on context
- Wordmark: Same height as logo mark
- Tagline: 50–60% of wordmark size

### 7.2 Spacing

| Element | Spacing |
|---------|---------|
| Logo mark to wordmark | 8–16px |
| Wordmark to tagline | 4–8px |
| Icon internal padding | Even stroke widths |

### 7.3 Alignment

- Logo mark and wordmark: Vertically centered
- Tagline: Left-aligned with wordmark or centered below logo group
- Icon: Geometric center alignment

## 8. Use Cases

### 8.1 Website Header

**Layout:** Logo mark + wordmark + tagline (optional)
**Size:** Logo mark 32–40px, wordmark 24–32px
**Position:** Left side of header
**Color:** White on dark header, or accent

### 8.2 App Icon

**Layout:** Logo mark only (no wordmark)
**Size:** 32×32 to 512×512
**Background:** Dark navy (`#0D1117`) or white
**Logo color:** Accent or monochrome

### 8.3 Favicon

**Layout:** Simplified logo mark
**Size:** 16×16, 32×32
**Detail:** Dot/star + minimal W strokes
**Color:** Single solid color

### 8.4 Social/Avatar

**Layout:** Logo mark only
**Size:** 48×48 to 400×400
**Background:** Dark navy or accent
**Logo color:** White

### 8.5 Print

**Layout:** Full logo (mark + wordmark + tagline)
**Color:** CMYK conversion of digital palette
**Size:** Depends on print size

## 9. Design Principles

### 9.1 Simplicity

- Reduce to essential strokes
- Every line must earn its place
- No decoration for decoration's sake
- Must work at 16×16 pixels

### 9.2 Timelessness

- Avoid trends
- Geometric foundation
- Clean lines
- Will age well over 10+ years

### 9.3 Versatility

- Works in dark and light
- Works in monochrome
- Works at all sizes
- Works in motion (if animated)

### 9.4 Governance Messaging

- Dot/star must be visible
- Direction must be clear
- Stability must be felt
- Trust must be conveyed
