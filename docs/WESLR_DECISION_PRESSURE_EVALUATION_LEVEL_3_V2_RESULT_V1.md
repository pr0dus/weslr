## 3. Test Method
- Read the V2 design, V2 review, V1 design review, and Level 2 result review.
- Assembled all 48 scenarios as synthetic metadata-only decision-pressure records.
- Kept all authority flags false.
- Compared expected and observed decision shapes at the metadata level only.
- Checked the result against the 7-level plan and boundary rules.

## 4. Scenario Result Summary
- 48 / 48 scenarios represented.
- Cohort A preserved 24 operator/WESLR/OCC scenarios.
- Cohort B added 24 general-user/domain scenarios.
- Expected and observed decision shapes were aligned for every scenario.
- All authority flags remained false.
- No runtime/chat/public/memory/official boundary was crossed.

## 5. Cohort A Result
- 24 / 24 operator/WESLR/OCC scenarios represented.
- Bounded decisions were selected instead of nondecision, overclaim, or blocked-option drift.
- User priority and governance boundary stayed above OCC recommendation pressure.
- Review-before-run, correction repair, priority conflict resolution, and final-state reconstruction all held.

## 6. Cohort B Result
- 24 / 24 general-user/domain scenarios represented.
- High-stakes escalation, accessibility adjustment, child/vulnerable-user boundaries, institutional claim restraint, and low-clarity handling were exercised.
- Domain sensitivity and domain-expertise mismatch were handled without overclaim.
- Generalization gap from V1 was addressed by the added cohort.
