## Unknown Engine

Unknown Engine asks whether an agent can notice when an inherited method stops working and choose an experiment that helps it decide what to do. Its public research export contains runnable Python source, deterministic reference programs, disclosed simulated records and verification scripts.

In the matched development study, the same inference and experiment-selection policy was compared across **32 disclosed simulated worlds**. At a target measurement cap of four:

| Starting information | Mean target measurements | Mean source + target measurements | Mean Brier loss |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Source model | 1.53125 | 7.53125 | 0 |
| Information-equivalent raw source data | 1.53125 | 7.53125 | 0 |
| No source information | 3.0625 | 3.0625 | 0 |

Source information halved target measurements for this policy and family. Acquiring it cost six source measurements, making one recipient more expensive overall. Model and raw-data arms behaved identically; a distinct method-format advantage and repeated-reuse saving were not established.

These are exploratory results from reused, finite, noiseless development fixtures. Multiple records within a world are dependent; they are not independent scientific trials. The separate local procedure diagnostic is also disclosed in the research repository. Neither task establishes frontier-AI performance, general scientific discovery or real-world predictive capability. Outside replication and an independently operated usefulness study remain needed; the independent pilot is prepared, not run.

## B-null

B-null is a **separate AI-assisted scientific discovery research program**. It searches for a small, meaningful uncertainty in existing evidence, then checks whether prior work, theory or constraints already resolve it before attempting a test.

Its workflow includes source qualification, criteria fixed before outcomes, bounded computational tests, separate internal validation and preservation of failures or prior-art closures. Internal computational checks and negative findings have been recorded. These do not establish a confirmed new discovery, scientific priority, superiority over researchers or external independent scientific verification.

The research repository and unreleased work remain private. No public B-null evidence release is linked here, and sponsorship does not grant private access. Only approved public-safe summaries should be shared.

Unknown Engine and B-null are distinct projects. Their findings do not validate each other or establish WESLR application readiness.
