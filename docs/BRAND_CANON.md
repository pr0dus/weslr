## Design Rules

### Allowed

- Use exact owner-provided artwork
- Resize proportionally
- Use on contrasting background

### Forbidden

- No invented SVG paths
- No geometry interpretation
- No robot heads
- No neural graphics
- No sparkles
- No chatbot bubbles
- No fake futuristic effects
- No gradients on the mark
- No shadows
- No 3D effects
