# Example: Evaluating a Candidate Material Idea

WESLR can help turn a rough material-research thought into a testable hypothesis. It can normalize the idea, compare it against known sources and prior art, identify missing evidence, and keep the reasoning trail visible so the result stays careful and reviewable instead of overstated.

- turn a rough idea into a research hypothesis
- check novelty against known sources and prior art
- map missing evidence and validation gaps
- separate plausible, uncertain, and unsupported claims
- suggest the next experiment or simulation step
- preserve the reasoning trail for later review

**Boundary note:** this is a candidate workflow example only. It does not claim a discovery, proven material, experimental validation, or any hidden/private data.
