# Weslr - Dietary & Nutrition Knowledge Base

## Common Food Calorie Reference

### Eggs
- **Large fried egg (1 egg, ~50g)**: ~90-100 calories
- **Large boiled egg**: ~78 calories
- **Large scrambled egg**: ~90 calories
- **Large poached egg**: ~74 calories
- **Large egg yolk only**: ~55 calories
- **Large egg white only**: ~17 calories
- **Protein per large egg**: ~6g
- **Fat per large egg**: ~6g (2g saturated)
- **Cholesterol per large egg**: ~185mg

### Protein Sources
- **Chicken breast (100g cooked)**: ~165 calories, 31g protein
- **Lean beef (100g cooked)**: ~250 calories, 26g protein
- **Salmon (100g cooked)**: ~208 calories, 20g protein
- **Tuna (100g canned in water)**: ~132 calories, 29g protein
- **Greek yogurt (170g)**: ~100 calories, 17g protein
- **Cottage cheese (100g)**: ~98 calories, 11g protein
- **Lentils (100g cooked)**: ~116 calories, 9g protein
- **Tofu (100g)**: ~76 calories, 8g protein

### Carbohydrates
- **White rice (100g cooked)**: ~130 calories
- **Brown rice (100g cooked)**: ~111 calories
- **Oats (40g dry)**: ~150 calories, 5g fiber
- **Whole wheat bread (1 slice)**: ~70 calories
- **Potato (150g baked)**: ~130 calories, 4g fiber
- **Sweet potato (150g baked)**: ~130 calories, 4g fiber
- **Banana (118g)**: ~105 calories, 3g fiber
- **Apple (182g)**: ~95 calories, 4g fiber

### Healthy Fats
- **Avocado (100g)**: ~160 calories, 15g fat
- **Almonds (28g/1 oz)**: ~164 calories, 14g fat
- **Walnuts (28g/1 oz)**: ~185 calories, 18g fat
- **Olive oil (1 tbsp)**: ~119 calories, 14g fat
- **Peanut butter (2 tbsp)**: ~190 calories, 16g fat
- **Chia seeds (28g)**: ~138 calories, 9g omega-3

### Daily Calorie Guidelines (General)
- **Adult women**: 1,600-2,400 calories/day (based on activity)
- **Adult men**: 2,000-3,000 calories/day (based on activity)
- **Children (2-8)**: 1,000-2,000 calories/day
- **Children (9-13)**: 1,400-2,600 calories/day
- **Teens**: 1,600-3,200 calories/day (based on age, gender, activity)

### Hand-Portion Guide
- **Protein**: Palm-sized portion = ~25-30g protein
- **Carbs**: Cupped hand = ~30g carbs
- **Fats**: Thumb-sized = ~10-15g fat
- **Vegetables**: Two fists = ~4-5 servings

## Behavioral Applications for Weslr

### Dietary Tracking & Meal Planning
- **Food logging**: Calorie and nutrient tracking from purchases
- **Meal planning**: Balanced meals based on dietary goals
- **Portion control**: Hand-portion estimation
- **Grocery list generation**: Nutrient-focused shopping
- **Recipe suggestions**: Based on available ingredients

### Health Monitoring
- **Hygiene reminders**: Hand washing, food safety
- **Nutrient gap detection**: Identifying missing vitamins/minerals
- **Calorie budgeting**: Daily intake tracking against goals
- **Meal timing**: Regular eating patterns for metabolism

### Family Wellness
- **Kid-friendly nutrition**: Age-appropriate portions, fun foods
- **Hand washing prompts**: Scheduled reminders, especially before meals
- **Healthy habit formation**: Positive reinforcement, consistency
- **Parental guidance**: Evidence-based nutrition recommendations

### Medical Integration
- **Diet-diagnosis tracking**: Food responses to conditions (diabetes, allergies)
- **Medication-nutrient interactions**: Calcium with antibiotics, etc.
- **Hydration tracking**: Water intake monitoring
- **Symptom correlation**: Diet patterns with health outcomes

### Implementation Requirements

#### Knowledge Base Needs
1. **Food database**: Calorie/nutrient information per food item
2. **Dietary guidelines**: Age-, condition-, and goal-specific recommendations
3. **Allergy database**: Food allergens and cross-contamination risks
4. **Cultural preferences**: Dietary restrictions based on beliefs
5. **Budget considerations**: Cost-effective healthy food options

#### Mathematical Models
1. **Calorie tracking**: Linear accumulation with daily caps
2. **Nutrient saturation**: Logistic growth (too much can be harmful)
3. **Habit formation**: Repetition + reinforcement = automatic behavior
4. **Reminder timing**: Cron scheduling for hand washing before meals
5. **Goal progress**: Trajectory analysis toward health objectives

#### Safety & Ethics
1. **No medical advice**: General nutrition guidance only
2. **Calorie warnings**: Special considerations for children, elderly
3. **Allergy alerts**: Clear communication of risks
4. **Cultural sensitivity**: Respect for dietary restrictions
5. **User control**: Opt-in/out of all features
