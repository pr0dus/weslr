# CircleForge - Religious References

## Major World Religions

### The "Big Five" World Religions

**Christianity** (≈2.4 billion adherents)
- Core: Jesus Christ as Son of God, salvation through faith
- Branches: Catholicism, Orthodoxy, Protestantism
- Key texts: Bible (Old and New Testaments)
- Practices: Baptism, Eucharist, prayer, worship
- Ethical framework: Love, forgiveness, service

**Islam** (≈1.9 billion adherents)
- Core: Submission to Allah, Muhammad as final prophet
- Five Pillars: Shahada, Salat, Zakat, Sawm, Hajj
- Key texts: Quran, Hadith
- Branches: Sunni (85-90%), Shia (10-15%)
- Ethical framework: Justice, compassion, community

**Hinduism** (≈1.2 billion adherents)
- Core: Dharma, karma, moksha (liberation)
- Key concepts: Brahman (ultimate reality), Atman (self)
- Major deities: Vishnu, Shiva, Devi, Brahma
- Key texts: Vedas, Upanishads, Bhagavad Gita
- Practices: Puja, yoga, meditation, festivals
- Ethical framework: Right action, duty, non-harm (ahimsa)

**Buddhism** (≈500 million adherents)
- Core: Four Noble Truths, Eightfold Path, impermanence
- Key concepts: Suffering, emptiness (sunyata), mindfulness
- Branches: Theravada, Mahayana, Vajrayana
- Key texts: Pali Canon, Mahayana Sutras
- Practices: Meditation, mindfulness, ethical conduct
- Ethical framework: Compassion, non-attachment, wisdom

**Judaism** (≈15 million adherents)
- Core: Covenant with God, Torah as guide
- Key concepts: Monotheism, chosen people, messianic age
- Key texts: Tanakh (Hebrew Bible), Talmud
- Branches: Orthodox, Conservative, Reform, Reconstructionist
- Practices: Shabbat, holidays, kosher, mitzvot
- Ethical framework: Justice, compassion, study

### Other Major World Religions

**Baháʼí Faith** (≈7 million adherents)
- Core: Unity of all religions, unity of humanity
- Key figures: Báb, Baháʼu'lláh, ʻAbdu'l-Bahá
- Key texts: Kitáb-i-Aqdas, Kitáb-i-Íqán
- Principles: One God, one religion, one humanity
- Practices: Prayer, meditation, service

**Sikhism** (≈25 million adherents)
- Core: One God, equality, service
- Key figures: Guru Nanak, ten Gurus
- Key texts: Guru Granth Sahib
- Practices: Nitnem (daily prayers), Langar (community meal)
- Symbols: Five Ks (Kesh, Kara, Kanga, Kachera, Kirpan)

**Jainism** (≈4.5 million adherents)
- Core: Ahimsa (non-violence), Anekantavada (multiplicity of views)
- Key figures: Mahavira, 24 Tirthankaras
- Practices: Fasting, meditation, asceticism
- Ethics: Non-harm, truth, non-stealing, celibality, non-possession

**Taoism (Daoism)** (≈20-30 million adherents)
- Core: Tao (Way), Wu-wei (non-action), simplicity
- Key texts: Tao Te Ching, Zhuangzi
- Practices: Meditation, alchemy, festivals
- Ethics: Harmony with nature, humility, compassion

### Indigenous and Folk Religions

- **Shinto** (Japan) - Nature spirits (kami), ancestor veneration
- **Traditional African Religions** - Ancestor veneration, nature spirits
- **Native American Religions** - Sacred land, vision quests, animal spirits
- **Australian Aboriginal Religions** - Dreamtime, sacred sites

### New Religious Movements

- **Scientology** - Technology of the mind
- **Rastafarianism** - Haile Selassie as messiah, Africa as Promised Land
- **Tenrikyo** - Joyful mind, construction of the Town of Perfect Happiness
